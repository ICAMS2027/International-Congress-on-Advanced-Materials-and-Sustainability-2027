Scientific Topics

1. Semiconductor Materials and Devices

Semiconductor synthesis, processing, doping, defects, heterostructures, electrical properties, semiconductor physics, and device applications.

2. Electronic and Optoelectronic Materials

Electronic materials, optoelectronic materials, optical and electrical properties, LEDs, photodetectors, sensors, and related devices.

3. Nanomaterials and Low-Dimensional Structures

Nanoparticles, nanowires, nanotubes, quantum dots, thin films, two-dimensional materials, and nanostructured systems.

4. Advanced Functional Materials

Dielectric, ferroelectric, piezoelectric, magnetic, multifunctional, and smart materials.

5. Materials for Energy Conversion and Storage

Solar cells, batteries, supercapacitors, fuel cells, energy harvesting, hydrogen technologies, and advanced materials for energy applications.

6. Photocatalytic and Photoactive Materials

Semiconductor photocatalysts, heterojunctions, photoelectrochemistry, solar-driven processes, pollutant degradation, and environmental remediation.

7. Sustainable and Green Materials Processing

Green synthesis, bio-assisted synthesis, sustainable precursors, low-energy processing, waste-derived materials, circular materials, and environmentally responsible fabrication.

8. Thin Films, Coatings and Interfaces

Thin-film deposition, functional coatings, surface engineering, interfaces, contacts, surface modification, and related characterization.

9. Polymers, Hybrid and Composite Materials

Conducting polymers, organic semiconductors, polymer composites, hybrid materials, and multifunctional composites.

10. Optical, Photonic and Laser Materials

Optical materials, photonic materials, nonlinear optical materials, luminescent materials, crystals, glasses, and laser materials.

11. Thermoelectric, Ionic and Emerging Materials

Thermoelectric materials, solid-state ionic materials, novel functional materials, and emerging materials technologies.

12. Materials Characterization & Structure–Property Relationships

Structural, morphological, optical, electrical, dielectric, magnetic, thermal, and surface characterization, including advanced microscopy, diffraction, and spectroscopy techniques.
