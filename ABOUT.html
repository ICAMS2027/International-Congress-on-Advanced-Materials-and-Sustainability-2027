About ICAMS 2027

The International Congress on Advanced Materials and Sustainability (ICAMS 2027) is an international scientific forum dedicated to recent advances in the synthesis, processing, characterization, properties, and applications of advanced materials.

ICAMS 2027 brings together researchers, academics, students, and professionals from universities, research centers, and industry to exchange knowledge and discuss emerging developments in materials science.

The congress places particular emphasis on electronic and optoelectronic materials, semiconductors, nanomaterials, functional materials, energy conversion and storage, photocatalysis, sustainable materials processing, and emerging technologies.

ICAMS 2027 will be held in Mazatlán, Sinaloa, Mexico, in a hybrid format, allowing both on-site and online participation.

Aims & Scope

ICAMS 2027 aims to promote international scientific collaboration and the exchange of knowledge in advanced materials science. The congress covers fundamental and applied research related to:

Synthesis → Processing → Characterization → Structure–Property Relationships → Applications

Special attention is given to materials and technologies with potential applications in electronics, optoelectronics, energy, environmental remediation, and sustainable development.
