Abstract Submission

Researchers, academics, professionals, and students are invited to submit abstracts presenting original research related to the scientific topics of ICAMS 2027.

Abstracts should clearly describe:

Material/System → Synthesis or Preparation → Characterization → Main Results → Properties → Potential Application

Presentation Categories

  • Oral Presentation — On-site
  • Oral Presentation — Online
  • Poster Presentation — On-site

Abstract Guidelines

Abstracts must be submitted in English. Each abstract should include:

  • Title
  • Authors & Affiliations
  • Corresponding Author
  • Abstract Body
  • Keywords
  • Selected Scientific Topic
  • Preferred Presentation Format
