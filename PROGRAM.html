Scientific Program

The ICAMS 2027 scientific program will include plenary lectures, keynote lectures, invited talks, oral presentations, poster sessions, and special scientific sessions.

Scientific Sessions

  • Session I — Semiconductor Materials and Devices
  • Session II — Electronic and Optoelectronic Materials
  • Session III — Nanomaterials and Low-Dimensional Structures
  • Session IV — Advanced Functional Materials
  • Session V — Materials for Energy Conversion and Storage
  • Session VI — Photocatalytic and Photoactive Materials
  • Session VII — Sustainable and Green Materials Processing
  • Session VIII — Thin Films, Coatings and Interfaces
  • Session IX — Polymers, Hybrid and Composite Materials
  • Session X — Optical, Photonic and Laser Materials
  • Session XI — Thermoelectric, Ionic and Emerging Materials
  • Session XII — Materials Characterization and Structure–Property Relationships
